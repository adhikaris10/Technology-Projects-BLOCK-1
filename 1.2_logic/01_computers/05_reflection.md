# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?
  
 During this lab, I learned many interesting things about computer hardware and how different components work together to make a computer function. I was surprised to discover that a computer contains many small but important components, such as the CPU, RAM, motherboard, storage, power supply and cooling system. Each component has its own function, and all of them work together to perform different tasks.

Some components that were new to me were GPIO pins, SoC, HDMI and the microSD card used in the Raspberry Pi. Before this lab, I did not know much about Raspberry Pi hardware or how it works as an embedded computer. I learned that GPIO pins can connect external devices such as sensors, LEDs and motors.

One of the challenges I faced during this lab was identifying different hardware components and understanding their functions. Sometimes, it was difficult to recognise the components because they looked similar. However, by studying diagrams, images and explanations, I gradually improved my understanding.

I also learned that embedded systems are small computer systems designed to perform specific tasks. The Raspberry Pi is a good example because it combines many components on a single board. This makes it compact, affordable and useful for different projects, including robotics, automation and smart devices.

Overall, this lab was a valuable learning experience. It improved my knowledge of computer hardware, Raspberry Pi and embedded systems. I also gained practical knowledge that will help me in my future studies of computer science.
